# LN reading surfaces

Mode: Read. Scope: library `/`, novel contents `/[novel]/`, chapter reader. User approved direct build of an LN contents-page system with ink-violet navigation, neutral library ground, and preserved saved reading settings. No sales or marketing sections; no new product features.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A digital edition you can open at the right chapter, not a promotional site. The library starts with real titles and reading continuity; the reader starts with location, chapter title and prose.

OWN-WORLD: Ink violet navigation and numbering, cool neutral library sheets, clear Be Vietnam Pro interface, Noto Serif only for narrative prose. Compact rounded controls, open lists, generous but bounded margins. The reading column retains all four existing presets.

STORY: Find a title, select its Arc, enter a chapter, continue where you stopped. Preserve URL and localStorage contracts. Saved reading is marked with text as well as color. Normal reading has no controls floating over prose. Explicitly opened settings is a modal reading pause with backdrop, scroll lock, keyboard containment and focus return, not a simultaneous reading state.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact masthead above a 1120px library with one real novel entry, its author and Arc links. Novel contents have a compact introduction and Arc navigation alongside open chapter rows. Reader uses a quiet header, restrained chapter opener and a single readable column; no promotional hero.

FORM: Grounded candidate 6, LN volume contents leaf, seed c0cb7152. Other grounded systems: library catalogue, serial issue index, chapter navigation folio, paperback colophon, e-reader shelf, and reading-room journal. Catalog challengers declined: brick instructions (clarity of sequence retained), provenance ribbon (explicit uncertainty retained), cathode gauze (visible chosen state retained), cutting bench (resume marker retained), broadside (typographic hierarchy retained), marketing monochrome (grid discipline retained, claim/proof topology rejected by product brief). These are disciplines, not borrowed costumes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Build path: code-led; no image-generation tool available. No sourced cover selected or shipped; use honest title treatment, not invented publisher artwork.
